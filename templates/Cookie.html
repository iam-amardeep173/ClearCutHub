🍪 Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how ClearCutHub ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us improve your experience, understand how our website is used, and personalize content.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for website functionality (e.g., login, page navigation).
  • Performance Cookies: Help us analyze how visitors use our site.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember preferences and enhance features.
  • Marketing Cookies: Track your visits to help deliver relevant ads.

3. How You Can Control Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings. Note that disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services such as Google Analytics, Facebook, or advertising partners.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with the date of the latest revision.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, feel free to contact us or email us at support@clearcuthub.com.
